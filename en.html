MORI PRIVACY POLICY

At a glance
Your study notes and goals stay on your device. Mori has no ads, account requirement or behavioral analytics. You may enable local reminders. Optional Mori Atelier purchases use the Apple App Store or Google Play; Mori never sees your card details. The sections below explain what happens to your data, your choices and your rights.

1. Who is responsible and how to contact us
This policy covers Mori for iOS and Android, bundle/package app.mori.mori. Publisher and privacy contact: Taha Bayar (Mori / Lumina Digitale). Email: luminadigitale@gmail.com. You can put “Mori Privacy” in the subject of a privacy or support request. Publisher and contact details will be kept consistent with the store account.

2. Information kept on your device
Mori saves your notes, boards, goal-wall day and week progress, titles, colors, fonts, language and motion preferences, focus/break durations and completed-session count in the app’s local storage. These are preferences and content you choose to create. The app does not upload these files to a developer server; we cannot remotely read your notes or study habits. Save files do not have additional application-level encryption. Your device lock, operating system protections and backup settings matter.

3. Permissions, notifications and widget
If you set a reminder or focus completion alert, the operating system may request notification permission. It is optional; the main clock, board and timer work without it. Alerts are scheduled on your device. Delivery can be delayed by battery management or operating-system behavior; Mori is not an emergency alarm. The keep-awake option affects your device and may increase battery use. A note or progress added to the Android home-screen widget may be visible to someone who can see your device, depending on lock-screen and launcher settings; avoid sensitive notes there if you prefer. There is no iOS home-screen widget in this version. Mori does not request camera, microphone, contacts or location access. If you enable weather and choose a city, the app sends that city's coordinates to the api.met.no forecast service. That request may be visible to the service alongside your IP address; your notes, goals, and precise device location are not sent. The service's own privacy terms govern its handling of the request.

4. How purchases work
The optional Mori Atelier Pack is a one-time digital product with ID mori_lifetime_all. The purchase screen loads its current price and currency from the Apple App Store or Google Play. The store confirmation screen controls the actual price and tax. The store processes payment. You do not enter a card number, bank account or billing address into Mori, and the developer does not receive them. To unlock and restore the pack, the app receives the product ID, purchase state and transaction proof from the store on your device. This version does not send that proof to a developer server. Stores process transaction information under their own policies. Refunds, cancellations and account billing are handled through the relevant store. Purchasing requires a store account and internet connection; the core study features do not.

5. Support email
If you email us from your own email app, we receive your address, message and any attachment you intentionally provide. We use them to answer your request, follow up and maintain necessary records. Please avoid sending private notes, health details, payment card details or sensitive information about children. Messages can pass through email providers and be processed outside your country. Support messages are deleted when no longer needed for the request and when no legal retention requirement applies. You may request access or deletion by emailing the same address. Mori does not automatically send crash reports, in-app support messages, advertising analytics or marketing email.

6. Sharing and international processing
We do not sell your notes or usage history, share them with data brokers or AI services, or analyze them for advertising. When the app opens, it may contact Apple or Google store services to check the pack price and existing purchase entitlement. These services also handle purchases or restorations you initiate. Stores and support email providers may use infrastructure in different countries. This does not mean your local notes are sent to a developer server. If disclosure is legally required, we will follow the applicable process.

7. Retention, deletion and backups
Local data stays on your device while you use the app. You can edit or delete notes and progress in the app. Removing Mori or clearing its storage in device settings may remove local records. This version has no user-facing import/export tool; keep separate copies of important notes. Android app backup is disabled. iOS system backup behavior depends on your iCloud and local backup settings; manage those through Apple device/account settings. Store purchase records remain with Apple or Google separately; deleting Mori does not automatically delete a store transaction. You can ask us to delete support correspondence separately.

8. Legal grounds and your rights
Mori processes essential local information on your device to provide the study and customization features you request. Optional alerts follow your preference and OS permission. We process support messages only as needed to respond and handle rights requests. Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR, UK GDPR, Türkiye’s KVKK or other privacy rules may give you rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing and, where applicable, obtain data portability. California and other regional laws may provide rights to know, correct, delete or opt out of sale or targeted advertising. Mori does not sell user data or show targeted ads. To make a request, email luminadigitale@gmail.com. We may request reasonable information to verify that a request is yours. We cannot remotely access or delete a note that only exists on your device; you can manage it there. Local app content and store transaction records are held in separate systems.

9. Children, security and automated decisions
Mori is a general-purpose study-planning tool; it is not designed or marketed specifically for children. It does not collect birthdates or identity documents for age checks. If we learn that a support message contains a child’s personal information, we will review and take appropriate deletion steps. For local data, Mori relies on the operating system’s app isolation and your device security. We cannot promise absolute security or zero data loss. Mori does not profile users for ads or make automated decisions with significant effects on individuals.

10. Changes
If a future feature uploads notes, introduces ads or analytics, or changes purchase-data flows, this policy and store declarations will be updated first, with in-app notice when required. The date at the top identifies the current version. Contact: luminadigitale@gmail.com.
